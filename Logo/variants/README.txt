Srivastava Lab logo — variants
==============================

Nothing in here is used by the website. These are spares for other contexts.
The three files the site actually loads live one level up:

  favicon.ico                  browser tab icon
  srivastava-lab-mark.svg      modern favicon
  srivastava-lab-mark-256.png  Apple touch icon

Note that the masthead and nav logos are NOT loaded from a file — the SVG is
written directly into each of the eight HTML pages, so changing the on-page
logo means editing the markup, not swapping an image here.


slides/  — THE ONE YOU PROBABLY WANT
------------------------------------

The logo together with "Srivastava Lab" and the department line, sized for
presentations and posters, light and dark versions. See slides/README.txt.

Everything below is just the mark on its own, no name.


Mark-only variants in this folder
---------------------------------

  srivastava-lab-mark-mono.svg     Single-colour version. For faxes, embroidery,
                                   laser etching, or printing where colour is
                                   not available.

  srivastava-lab-mark-reverse.svg  Light-on-dark version, for placing on a dark
  srivastava-lab-mark-reverse-128.png   background or a photograph.
  srivastava-lab-mark-reverse-512.png

  srivastava-lab-mark-simple.svg   Fewer rays and thicker strokes, so it stays
  srivastava-lab-mark-simple-16.png     legible at very small sizes.
  srivastava-lab-mark-simple-32.png

  srivastava-lab-mark-64.png       Raster exports of the full mark, for places
  srivastava-lab-mark-128.png      that won't take an SVG (some conference
  srivastava-lab-mark-512.png      submission forms, Word, older software).

Prefer the .svg wherever it is accepted — it stays sharp at any size.

Colours: amber #c9812f, teal #0f6e78, near-black #111820, disc #e8f2f2.
